About UniBook

UniBook is an advanced university facility reservation and operational management platform engineered with Python, Django 6, Django REST Framework, and MySQL 8.

Our Mission

University campuses host hundreds of high-value resources: state-of-the-art supercomputing laboratories, seminar complexes, auditoriums, Olympic-standard swimming pools, sports courts, and student dining facilities. UniBook replaces fragmented manual register logs, email chains, and double-booking disputes with an automated, synchronized, real-time platform.

Core Architectural Highlights

Conflict Prevention Engine

Employs atomic database transactions with row-level locks (select_for_update()) to guarantee zero double-bookings.

Cryptographic QR Entry Passes

Every confirmed booking generates a tamper-evident QR code token verified in milliseconds by facility entrance scanners.

Multi-Tier Role-Based Access

Distinct, secure portals for Students, Faculty Members, Facility Managers, and Super Administrators.

Real-Time Utilization Analytics

Dynamic Chart.js visualizations for peak hour demand, facility occupancy rates, and revenue exportable to CSV.
